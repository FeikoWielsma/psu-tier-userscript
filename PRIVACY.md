# Privacy Policy for PSU Tier Badges

**Last updated:** June 15, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the **PSU Tier Badges** browser extension handles your data.

## 1. No Data Collection
**PSU Tier Badges** does not collect, store, or transmit any user data.
* **No personal data:** The extension does not access, collect, or store personal information (such as names, email addresses, or IP addresses).
* **No browsing history:** While the extension runs on PCPartPicker and Tweakers.net listings, it does not track, save, or upload your browsing history.
* **No analytics:** The extension does not contain any tracking codes, telemetry, or analytics platforms.

## 2. Local Processing
All matching logic and badge rendering are executed entirely locally in your web browser. 
* The database containing PSU ratings is built-in directly to the extension package. 
* The extension reads power supply product names on the matching web pages to find corresponding ratings and injects the rating badges into the page's DOM. 
* No data is sent over the network to any external servers.

## 3. Third-Party Websites
This extension enhances PCPartPicker and Tweakers.net. We are not affiliated with these websites, and their respective privacy policies govern your usage of their platforms.
